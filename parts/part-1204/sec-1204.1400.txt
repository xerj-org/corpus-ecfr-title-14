Title 14 CFR — Part 1204—ADMINISTRATIVE AUTHORITY AND POLICY
Subpart 14—Use of NASA Airfield Facilities by Aircraft Not Operated for the Benefit of the Federal Government
## § 1204.1400 Scope.

§ 1204.1400 Scope.

This subpart establishes the responsibility and sets forth the conditions and procedures for the use of NASA airfield facilities by aircraft not operated for the benefit of the Federal Government.
