Title 14 CFR — Part 25—AIRWORTHINESS STANDARDS: TRANSPORT CATEGORY AIRPLANES
Subpart H—Electrical Wiring Interconnection Systems (EWIS)
## § 25.1719 Accessibility provisions: EWIS.

§ 25.1719 Accessibility provisions: EWIS.

Access must be provided to allow inspection and replacement of any EWIS component as necessary for continued airworthiness.
