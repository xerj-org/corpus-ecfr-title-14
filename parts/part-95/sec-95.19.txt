Title 14 CFR — Part 95—IFR ALTITUDES
Subpart B—Designated Mountainous Areas
## § 95.19 Hawaii Mountainous Area.

§ 95.19 Hawaii Mountainous Area.

The following islands of the State of Hawaii: Kauai, Oahu, Molokai, Lanai, Kehoolawe, Maui, and Hawaii.
