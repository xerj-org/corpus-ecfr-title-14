Title 14 CFR — Part 93—SPECIAL AIR TRAFFIC RULES
Subpart D—Anchorage, Alaska, Terminal Area
## § 93.51 Applicability.

§ 93.51 Applicability.

This subpart prescribes special air traffic rules for aircraft operating in the Anchorage, Alaska, Terminal Area.
